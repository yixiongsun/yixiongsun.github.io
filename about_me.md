# About

I am a systems neuroscience PhD candidate and software engineer interested in how biological and artificial systems learn from experience, build internal models of the world, and use them to guide behavior.

My research focuses on how memories are encoded, consolidated, and retrieved through changing patterns of neural activity. I combine *in vivo* electrophysiology with machine learning, signal processing, and computational modeling to analyze noisy, high-dimensional neural data.

I also have experience building production software across databases, backend and frontend systems, mobile applications, cloud infrastructure, and DevOps. This combination of neuroscience and engineering shapes my interest in intelligent systems: how they learn, adapt, and arrive at their decisions.

## Background

- Systems neuroscience and *in vivo* electrophysiology
- Machine learning and signal processing for neural time-series data
- Computational modeling and quantitative analysis
- Full-stack, mobile, cloud, and production software engineering
